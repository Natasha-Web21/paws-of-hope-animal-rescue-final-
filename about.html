Who We Are

Paws of Hope Animal Rescue is a community-focused non-profit organisation dedicated to helping abandoned, neglected and vulnerable animals.

We believe that every animal deserves care, protection and the opportunity to find a loving home.

Our Mission

Our mission is to rescue animals in need, provide them with temporary care and connect them with responsible families.

Our Main Goals

  • Rescue animals that need protection.
  • Provide safe temporary shelter and care.
  • Promote responsible pet ownership.
  • Find suitable forever homes for rescued animals.
  • Educate the community about animal welfare.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a community where animals are treated with kindness, compassion and respect.

We aim to help create a future where fewer animals are abandoned and more animals have safe and loving homes.

Our Values

  1. Compassion
  2. Responsibility
  3. Respect for animals
  4. Community involvement
  5. Commitment to animal welfare
