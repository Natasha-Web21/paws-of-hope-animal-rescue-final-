Animal Adoption

Paws of Hope helps rescued animals find responsible and caring forever homes.

Our adoption process focuses on finding a suitable match between each animal and its potential family.

Our Services

  • Animal rescue and temporary care
  • Pet adoption support
  • Animal welfare education
  • Responsible pet ownership information
  • Community animal welfare support

Adoption Steps

  1. Browse the animals available for adoption.
  2. Submit an adoption enquiry.
  3. Discuss your suitability with the organisation.
  4. Prepare your home for the animal.
  5. Welcome your new companion into your home.

Why Adopt?

Adoption gives a vulnerable animal an opportunity to experience a safe and caring home.

By adopting, you can help reduce the number of animals waiting for permanent homes & support animal welfare in the community.
